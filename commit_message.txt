🎨 Palette: Add aria-pressed to PresentationModal toggles

💡 What: Added `aria-pressed` attributes to the "Smart Animate" and "Autoplay" toggle buttons in `PresentationModal`.
🎯 Why: State toggle buttons require explicit `aria-pressed` state to be accurately announced by screen readers, replacing potentially ambiguous implied states.
📸 Before/After: No visual change. (Accessibility only)
♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader announcements for presentation playback controls by explicitly communicating their toggled active/inactive state.
