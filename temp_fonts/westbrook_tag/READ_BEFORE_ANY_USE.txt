Westbrook Tag is a fast marker handstyle, drawn the way a name is signed across a wall in one pass.

Stroke width stays roughly even but the letters pick up sudden slashes and drag-out tails that cross the next form. There is a real lean to the alphabet and a deliberate looseness — letters are not lined up to a baseline so much as ridden along it. The lowercase carries the most movement; the caps stretch into long horizontal tags with crossbars that overshoot.

Belongs on the steel shutter of a closed shopfront after midnight, or scrawled in black on a courier sticker before it gets slapped on a lamppost.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
