Shorebreak Script is a bold brush handletter built on confident, energetic strokes and a slight right slant.

The capitals carry the weight of a thick-loaded paintbrush, while the lowercase joins together in a steady flow that keeps the rhythm of fast handwriting. The strokes feel direct rather than ornate — no extra flourishes, just the swing and grit of marker work done at speed. It reads loudest at large sizes, where the character of each stroke can breathe.

The lettering belongs on a hand-painted longboard, a sun-bleached enamel sign at the end of a coastal road, the spine of a 7-inch sleeve, or the side of a wooden crate set down on a wharf at first light.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
